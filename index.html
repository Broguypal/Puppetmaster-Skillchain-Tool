⚙ PUP SOLO SKILLCHAIN VISUALIZER

FFXI Puppetmaster — Master H2H ↔ Automaton WS • Proper Inhibitor/maneuver mechanics  •  By Broguypal

MECHANICS REFERENCE
No Attachments: Auto WSes freely. No waiting, no SC awareness. Uses maneuver-determined WS.
Inhibitor Only: Auto waits (master TP≥~900). SC awareness. Picks highest-priority WS that can form any SC. SC level is ignored. Maneuvers ignored for closing.
Speedloader Only: Auto waits. SC awareness. Picks WS for highest SC level (using first-match resolution). Ties broken by SC result ordering, then priority.
Both: Auto waits. Highest SC level (first-match). Ties by SC result ordering, then priority. Speedloader's behavior wins.
Opening WS: All modes use maneuver-determined WS for opening (no prior WS to chain with).
Maneuver rule: Max 3 active (can repeat). Auto picks WS whose element has most stacks. Ties → highest priority WS wins. Elements not mapped to any frame WS are wasted.
SC Resolution: Both selection and resolution use first-match property order (matching Lua check_props). Ties broken by SC result index (Light→Darkness→Gravitation→...), then priority.
LEVEL 4 / ULTIMATE SKILLCHAINS
Lv4 Light ("double light"): Light (WS property or Light skillchain) → Light-property WS = Level 4 Light. Available to anyone, no aeonic weapon required. Cannot be continued by any further WS — it closes the chain. Same for Lv4 Darkness ("double darkness") via Darkness → Darkness-property WS.
Radiance / Umbra (Ultimate SC): Same Light→Light / Darkness→Darkness trigger, but the Master must have Godhands equipped with Aeonic Aftermath active when closing. Minimum chain length depends on AM level: AM3 = closer can be the 2nd WS (Light WS → Light WS) • AM2 = closer must be the 3rd WS or later (any SC → Light SC → Light WS) • AM1 = closer must be the 4th WS or later. Below the threshold, the result is still a Lv4 double Light/Darkness — just not Radiance/Umbra.
Shijin Spiral bonus property: While Godhands Aeonic Aftermath is active, Shijin Spiral gains an extra skillchain property: Light (on top of its normal Fusion/Reverberation). This lets Shijin Spiral itself open or close Light→Light chains.
OTHER NOTES
Weapon-gated WS: Dragon Blow = Dragon Fangs. Maru Kala = Varga Purnikawa. Only one weapon at a time.
🥊 Master Only mode: Shows all skillchains achievable by the Master WSing repeatedly with themselves. Attachments and maneuvers are irrelevant.
🤖 Auto Only mode: Shows all skillchains the Automaton can form with itself. Without attachments, auto repeats the same WS (limited SCs). Inhibitor/Speedloader enable dynamic WS selection for complex chains.
